# Vin Paradise Unisex Salon & Spa - PRD

## Problem Statement
Build a premium dark-themed spa & salon website for "Vin Paradise Unisex Salon & Spa – Nagarbhavi" with dark luxury design, gold accents, WhatsApp/Call CTAs throughout, service cards with popup modals, and full mobile responsiveness.

## Architecture
- **Frontend Only**: React single-page website (no backend)
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS with custom dark theme
- **Components**: Shadcn UI (Dialog, ScrollArea)
- **Fonts**: Playfair Display (headings) + DM Sans (body)

## What's Been Implemented (Dec 2025)
- Fixed navbar with 6 nav links, logo, Call+WhatsApp buttons, mobile hamburger menu
- Hero section with full-screen background, heading, 3 CTA buttons
- Services section: 6 service cards with images, duration tabs, dynamic pricing, CTA buttons
- Service popup modal with image slider, benefits, products, what to expect, sticky CTA
- Premium Services: 2 large cards with PREMIUM badge and extra details
- About Us section with Kannada heading, stats
- Why Choose Us: 6 feature cards
- Gallery: 6 images with hover effects and lightbox
- Testimonials: 4 review cards with star ratings
- Contact: Address, phone, hours, Google Map embed
- Final CTA section
- Footer: 4 columns with all links
- Floating WhatsApp (pulsing glow) and Call buttons
- Mobile sticky bottom bar
- Smooth scroll navigation
- Navbar shrink on scroll
